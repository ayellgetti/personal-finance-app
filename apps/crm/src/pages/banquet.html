Use case · Banquet hall

WhatsApp enquiry to a booked wedding — then token, menu, décor, and event-day spend.

Priya Sharma messages the hall’s Business WhatsApp. Sales agent Ravi runs the eight CRM stages. If they book, he Converts. After that the work is clients, payments, tasks, and calendar — not a ninth pipeline stage.

Eight stages only

Do not add Menu, Token, or Décor as enquiry statuses. Those happen after Convert.

1 New 2 Contacted 3 Qualified 4 Discussion 5 Quotation Sent 6 Negotiation 7 Site Visit 8 Closed

WhatsApp is the source field. There is no WhatsApp sync in this product.

Happy path — wedding booked

Message: “Need hall for daughter’s wedding, 12 Dec, ~350 guests, vegetarian + Jain, budget around 8–10 lakh.”

  1. 1
    New — capture the WhatsApp

    Contacts → create Priya Sharma, type lead, mobile = WhatsApp number. Enquiries → title Wedding — 12 Dec 2026 — ~350 pax, source WhatsApp Business, status new, expected value ₹9,00,000, assigned to Ravi.

  2. 2
    Contacted — first call

    Ravi confirms the date is free and they want a sit-down dinner. Status → contacted. Follow-up 1 due tomorrow: “Family to choose lawn vs indoor.”

  3. 3
    Qualified — it fits

    350 pax fits Grand Lawn, 12 Dec is open, 8–10L is inside package range. Status → qualified. Expected value ₹9,50,000. If it did not fit, close here — do not Convert.

  4. 4
    Discussion — 2–3 follow-ups

    Status → discussion. Lock menu, décor, rooms, parking in enquiry notes. Three follow-ups: menu PDF, Jain count, GSTIN for billing.

  5. 5
    Quotation Sent

    Quote on WhatsApp: hall + menu + décor ₹9.5L + GST, 25% token to block the date. Status → quotation_sent. Store quote ref Q-2026-118 in notes (PDF is not stored in CRM).

  6. 6
    Negotiation

    Include entrance décor, DJ power in. Offer ₹9.2L all-in. Status → negotiation, expected value ₹9,20,000.

  7. 7
    Schedule Meeting / Site Visit

    Status → schedule_meeting. Calendar event: dusk walk of Grand Lawn. Task for Meera: décor sample board. Follow-up next morning for the decision.

  8. 8
    Closed — Convert (Booked)

    Priya: “Book it.” Hit Convert, not only Closed. That sets closedReason: Booked, contact type → client, and creates the Client row. Without Convert there is no client and no payments.

What goes in enquiry notes

There is no menu, décor, or venue inventory table. The requirement sheet lives on the enquiry.

After Convert — ops, not a new enquiry

Follow-ups can still be logged on the same closed enquiry. Payments need the client that Convert created.

Follow-ups and tasks

Menu tasting 20 Nov. Décor mock-up 5 Dec. Confirm 4 deluxe rooms. Calendar event for 12 Dec 4pm–12am.

Payments

Token and balance are INCOME. Extra florist or generator on the day are EXPENSE. Link enquiryId so the wedding still shows on the original deal.

When they do not book

Close the enquiry with a reason. Do not Convert. Contact stays a lead.

Reject

Not a fit at qualify

Birthday for 40 people, budget ₹25,000 vs 150 pax minimum. Status closed, reason Lost: below minimum pax / budget.

Lost

Booked another venue

Visit done, then Hotel X. Status closed, reason Lost: booked another venue. No token payment.

Dropped

Event postponed

Reason Lost: event postponed / dropped. If they return later, open a new enquiry on the same contact.

This walkthrough is a sample. WhatsApp, quotation PDFs, hall inventory, and menu SKUs are not modules in this CRM. Capture them in contact / enquiry notes, tasks, and calendar. Pipeline stages are fixed: do not add extras.
