Happy path — household onboarded

WhatsApp: “I saw myfinancefreedom.com. Can this show if I can retire at 50? I have a home loan and two SIPs.”

  1. 1
    New — capture the inbound

    Contact Kavya Iyer, type lead. Enquiry title Freedom Planner — Kavya Iyer — FIRE 50, source WhatsApp Business (or myfinancefreedom.com if they used the site form). Expected value = sample annual plan fee. Assigned Deepak.

  2. 2
    Contacted

    Call: salaried India, two dependents later, wants surplus and debt picture — not stock tips. Status → contacted. Follow-up: send the public /guide link so she can see Arjun’s sample before the demo.

  3. 3
    Qualified — audience match

    Product audience is salaried / self-employed adult in India, INR, household finance. She fits. Status → qualified. If they only want live trading or tax filing, close as not a fit — those are out of product scope.

  4. 4
    Discussion — 2–3 follow-ups

    Pain: “I do not know surplus after EMIs.” Follow-ups: she read /guide; she listed loans; she asked whether insurance is in the planner. Status → discussion. Notes hold what she wants from Quick Setup — CRM still does not store rupee amounts as finance data.

  5. 5
    Quotation Sent

    Sample commercial offer (illustrative, not a live SKU): annual Planner Assist ₹4,999 + optional 1:1 setup call ₹3,000. Status → quotation_sent. Quote ref in notes. Payment will be recorded in CRM after Convert, not in Freedom Planner.

  6. 6
    Negotiation

    Waive setup fee if she pays the annual plan on the demo call. Status → negotiation. Expected value ₹4,999.

  7. 7
    Schedule Meeting — product demo

    Status → schedule_meeting. Calendar: “Freedom Planner demo — Kavya Iyer”, 30 min screen-share. Walk /guide, then login page. Task: “Send calendar invite + /guide link”. This is the site-visit analogue.

  8. 8
    Convert — she pays and signs up

    She pays UPI on the call and creates her Freedom Planner account. Deepak hits Convert: enquiry Booked, contact → client, billing name Kavya Iyer. Then record the plan fee as Payment INCOME. Do not copy her salary into CRM.

CRM vs finance app

Two products · same login User, different jobs

Sales CRM · crm.local.uat

Contact, enquiry pipeline, follow-ups, Convert, plan-fee INCOME, ads EXPENSE, demo on calendar. Staff RBAC.

Freedom Planner · web.local.uat

Her income, EMIs, SIPs, insurance, Quick Setup, advisor, /guide. Owned by her userId. Not this CRM.

Same User identity can exist, but do not fold CRM into the finance app or store planner numbers on the enquiry.

After Convert — onboarding, not a new deal

Like menu and décor after a banquet booking: the sale is closed; the work is making the product stick.

Follow-up

Quick Setup complete

Due +3 days. Confirm she finished profile, emergency fund, and one FIRE path. Still on the closed enquiry.

Task

Advisor session

Help her open AI Advisor once numbers are in. Do not invent balances in CRM notes.

Calendar

Renewal reminder

11 months later: annual plan. New enquiry only if you treat renewal as a fresh sales cycle; otherwise a follow-up on this one.

When they do not buy

Reject

Wrong product

Wants live F&O tips, tax e-filing, or a shared household login. Close: Lost: out of product scope. Point them at /why if useful. No Convert.

Lost

Signed up without paying / used a competitor sheet

They created a free account and disappeared, or stayed on Excel. Reason Lost: no paid plan or Lost: stayed on spreadsheet.

Dropped

Not now

Reason Lost: deferred. Follow-up in 90 days as a new enquiry if you want a clean pipeline card.
