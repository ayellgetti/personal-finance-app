Happy path — 2BHK booked

Message: “Interested in 2BHK, Whitefield, budget 80–90L, need to move in 6 months.”

  1. 1
    New — capture the WhatsApp

    Contact Amit Patel, type lead. Enquiry title 2BHK Whitefield — 80-90L — 6mo occupancy, source WhatsApp Business, expected value ₹85,00,000, assigned Sneha.

  2. 2
    Contacted

    End-user (not investor), east-facing, ready-to-move or 6 months. Status → contacted. Follow-up: send brochure + price sheet.

  3. 3
    Qualified — a unit exists

    Tower B-1203, 2BHK, 1180 sqft, ₹84.5L. Status → qualified. If they want 3BHK at 2BHK price, close as lost — do not Convert.

  4. 4
    Discussion — 2–3 follow-ups

    Three options, they like B-1203. GST, corpus, car park. Father will co-apply for loan. Status → discussion.

  5. 5
    Quotation Sent

    Cost sheet on WhatsApp: ₹84.5L + GST, 10% token to block. Status → quotation_sent. Follow-up in 2 days.

  6. 6
    Negotiation

    Floor-rise waiver + extra parking at cost. Manager approval in notes. Status → negotiation.

  7. 7
    Site visit

    Status → schedule_meeting. Calendar: “Site visit B-1203 — Amit Patel”. Task for Karan: dummy key + sample flat. Follow-up next morning.

  8. 8
    Convert — Booked

    Amit pays token ₹8.45L. Convert: enquiry closed / Booked, contact → client, billing name Amit Patel (joint names in notes if needed).

After Convert — what maps from banquet

Banquet analogue

Menu tasting → cost-sheet / loan document chase. Décor → interiors or extra parking. Token → booking amount. Event-day expense → registration day.

Where it lives

Follow-ups on the closed enquiry. Tasks for agreement and loan file. Calendar for registration. Payments on the client.

When they do not book

Reject

Requirement / budget mismatch

Close at qualify: Lost: requirement / budget mismatch. No Convert.

Lost

Booked a competitor

Visit done. Reason Lost: booked competitor. Keep the contact as a lead for the next launch.

Dropped

Loan rejected / plan cancelled

Reason Lost: loan rejected or Lost: dropped purchase plan. If Convert already happened, refund the token as status refunded.

Sample rupees are illustrative. This CRM has no tower/unit inventory, cost-sheet PDF store, or WhatsApp sync. Put unit details in enquiry notes. Do not add extra pipeline stages.
