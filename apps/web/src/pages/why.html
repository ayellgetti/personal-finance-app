The problem we solve, the job of the app, and the financial ecosystem we are building.

Freedom Planner is not another expense tracker and not simply a financial product marketplace. It exists to help a household in India understand its complete financial picture — income, expenses, debt, insurance, investments, tax, goals and FIRE — and then take the right financial action.

Over time, Freedom Planner will connect customers with banks, mutual-fund providers, insurers and other financial partners. The plan comes first; the right financial product follows the need.

Read the problem Jump to the vision

Problem statement

Households already have the pieces of a financial life. They do not have one system that turns those pieces into answers they can use.

Exact problem: A salaried or self-employed adult in India cannot see, in one place, what is left after EMIs and SIPs, whether debt and insurance match their income and dependents, whether investments fund their goals and FIRE date, or which tax regime is cheaper for their year. Advice and product sales often happen without that complete picture.

Freedom Planner · the split picture we are replacing
Where it lives today What they still cannot see

EMIs in another app or Excel Debt burden, and which loan is the expensive one
SIPs on a broker or AMC site Whether the portfolio actually funds the dated goal

Tax in a CA spreadsheet or last year's return Old vs new regime with this year's deductions
Advice from relatives, YouTube, or a relationship manager A plan tied to their numbers, not a product quota

The problem is not "people do not have apps." They have too many fragments and no household financial decision system.

What that causes

Surplus, emergency months, insurance adequacy, and retirement year stay guesses. "Start a SIP", "buy term", or "take a loan" can become generic advice. A new job, EMI, dependent, or goal does not automatically update the financial plan.

What we are not solving first

We are not starting with live bank aggregation, PAN/Aadhaar KYC, tax filing, or a marketplace. Those can come later. The first job is to build a trusted financial picture and use it to make better decisions.

Objective of the app

Give the customer a living financial picture — then turn it into a forecast and a plan of action computed from their records, not guessed in a chat.

  1. 1
    Capture the household

    Profile, income, expenses, loans, investments, insurance, emergency fund, one FIRE path (Lean, Fat, or Coast), and other goals. Defaults: ₹, 6% inflation, retire at 60.

  2. 2
    Show one picture

    Net worth, surplus, debt-to-income, coverage, and goal progress on a dashboard. Bank or UPI statements become categorized lines. Tax compares old vs new for their year.

  3. 3
    Project and advise

    A deterministic cash-flow and net-worth engine produces the financial picture. A structured AI advisor explains that picture and identifies actions such as reducing expensive debt, completing the emergency fund, improving protection, or increasing investments.

  4. 4
    Eventually help execute the decision

    Once the customer has a validated need, Freedom Planner can connect them to the appropriate bank, mutual-fund provider, insurer, or other regulated financial partner and track the resulting product back inside the financial plan.

Who it is for

Salaried or self-employed adults in India who want FIRE, a house, education funding, better debt management, appropriate protection, or simply more control over their money.

What "done" means today

They finish setup, see a coherent dashboard from saved records, regenerate an advisor report when numbers change, and download a PDF.

How we stay honest

Planner math is the source of the picture. AI fills a structured schema on top of that math. We do not let a model invent surplus, EMI, coverage, or investment values.

Vision: From financial planner to financial decision platform

The long-term vision is bigger than tracking money. Freedom Planner becomes the financial decision layer between the customer and the financial ecosystem — understanding the customer's needs first, recommending the right action, and then helping the customer execute that decision through an appropriate partner.

The core principle: The product follows the need. The need does not follow the product. Freedom Planner should never recommend a loan, mutual fund, or insurance policy simply because it can earn a commission from it.

Freedom Planner · need → recommendation → partner

Need → product

Sell only what the financial plan already says the customer needs.

Cover

Insurance

If life or health protection is insufficient relative to income, dependents, liabilities, and goals, identify the protection gap and connect the customer with an appropriate insurance partner. If cover is already adequate, do not push another policy.

Goals

Mutual funds

If the emergency fund is complete, debt is manageable, and sustainable surplus remains, identify the investment need and help the customer start or increase goal-based investments through an appropriate investment partner.

Debt

Loans

If an existing loan is expensive and refinancing or a balance transfer genuinely improves the customer's position, connect them with an appropriate lending partner. If debt is already too heavy, the correct recommendation may simply be do not borrow.

Building the financial partner ecosystem

As the platform matures, Freedom Planner will integrate with multiple banks, NBFCs, mutual-fund providers, insurers, and other relevant financial institutions. The goal is not to promote one institution; it is to make the right product available when the customer's plan identifies a genuine need.

₹

Loan Partners

Banks and NBFCs for personal loans, home loans, business loans, refinancing, balance transfers, and other appropriate credit products.

↗

Investment Partners

Mutual-fund platforms, AMCs, and other investment partners for goal-based investing, SIPs, and long-term wealth creation.

+

Insurance Partners

Life, term, health, and other appropriate protection products where the customer's financial plan identifies a genuine coverage requirement.

Business model: a win-win financial ecosystem

Freedom Planner can build a sustainable business by helping customers make better financial decisions and earning revenue when those decisions result in suitable financial products being successfully purchased through partner institutions.

01

Customer gets value

The customer gets a complete financial picture, personalised recommendations, access to relevant financial products, and continued tracking of the impact of those decisions.

02

Partner gets qualified demand

Banks, mutual-fund providers, insurers, and other partners receive customers whose financial situation has already created a relevant need for their product.

03

Freedom Planner earns

When the customer successfully takes a suitable product through an eligible partner channel, Freedom Planner can earn the applicable commission, referral fee, trail, or distribution revenue.

The long-term Freedom Planner flywheel

Customer financial data → Financial analysis → Need identified → Right recommendation → Partner product → Customer action → Plan updated
Plan first. Product second. Customer outcome always first. The business succeeds when the customer succeeds — and the customer's financial plan remains the foundation of every recommendation.

The recommendation engine becomes the moat

Access to financial institutions alone is not the long-term advantage. The advantage is understanding the customer's complete financial situation before recommending a product.

Emergency fund incomplete

Prioritise building the cash buffer instead of pushing an aggressive investment product.

Debt burden too high

Prioritise reducing expensive debt or exploring a justified refinance. Additional borrowing may be inappropriate.

Protection gap exists

Prioritise appropriate insurance before increasing discretionary investments.

Surplus is healthy

Once the financial foundation is sound, identify appropriate goal-based investment opportunities.

Loan is expensive

Explore refinancing or balance transfer where the numbers justify the action.

No need exists

Tell the customer to continue their current plan. No product should be forced into the journey.

Trust is the foundation of the business

Commission-based distribution can only work long-term if the customer believes that recommendations are driven by their financial position, not by the commission available to Freedom Planner.

What we should always do

Explain why a product is being recommended, show the relevant financial gap or opportunity, compare appropriate alternatives where possible, and keep the customer's financial plan updated after the transaction.

What we should never do

Push a product because it pays more commission, recommend unnecessary debt, sell insurance when coverage is already adequate, or encourage investments before the customer's basic financial foundation is ready.

Regulatory principle: Any future distribution, referral, commission, advisory, or partner model must be implemented through the appropriate licensed entities, registrations, disclosures, agreements, and applicable Indian regulatory requirements. The product vision should not assume that every financial product can be distributed directly by Freedom Planner.

The ultimate vision

"What should I do with my money next?"

The customer should be able to ask Freedom Planner that question and receive an answer based on their actual financial life.

Freedom Planner understands the customer's income, spending, debt, insurance, investments, goals, tax position, emergency fund, and FIRE target. It calculates what matters, explains why, recommends the next appropriate action, and eventually helps the customer execute that action through the right financial partner.

That is the transition from a financial tracker to a financial decision platform — and ultimately to a trusted distribution ecosystem connecting customers with the right banks, mutual-fund providers, insurers, and other financial institutions.
